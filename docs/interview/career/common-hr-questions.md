# 24 câu hỏi HR / Hiring Manager thường gặp và cách trả lời

<div class="lesson-meta"><span>Behavioral & HR</span><span>English example answers</span><span>STAR(R) · Evidence-first</span></div>

Những câu sau thường xuất hiện ở các vòng recruiter, hiring manager và behavioral interview. **Tất cả câu trả lời là template hoặc tình huống mô phỏng**; không phải kinh nghiệm đã được xác nhận của người đọc.

## Career & motivation

### HR-01. Tell me about yourself.

**Cách trả lời:** Present → Past → Future, 45–90 giây. [Ví dụ và cách luyện](tell-me-about-yourself.md).

### HR-02. Walk me through your resume.

**Cách trả lời:** Nói sự chuyển biến qua 3–4 giai đoạn, tập trung 2 vai trò gần nhất. [Hướng dẫn](walk-through-resume.md).

### HR-03. Why are you looking for a new role?

**EN template:** “I've gained valuable experience in [real contribution]. I'm now looking for a role where I can contribute to [genuine next step]. The responsibilities here align with [specific JD aspect].”

**Đừng bịa:** lý do nghỉ việc, công ty đóng cửa, promotion, salary nếu không có thật.

### HR-04. Why do you want to work here?

**EN template:** “I noticed that your team is working on [verified product/engineering challenge]. My background in [relevant skill] could help with [role need]. I'd like to learn more about [real technical question].”

**Cần làm:** nghiên cứu JD, sản phẩm và stack thực tế, không thay mỗi tên công ty.

### HR-05. What is your biggest professional achievement?

**EN template:** “One project I'm proud of was [real project]. The challenge was [business impact]. I worked on [your action], and we verified the result through [tests or metrics].”

**Follow-up:** contribution cá nhân? metric trước/sau? Nếu không đo, không đặt số.

### HR-06. What are your career goals?

**EN template:** “I'd like to deepen my ability to design and operate dependable backend systems, while improving [genuine focus]. My goal is to keep making useful technical contributions and helping teams deliver safely.”

Không cần tự nhận tương lai chắc chắn làm Architect hoặc Manager.

### HR-07. Why should we hire you?

**EN template:** “I bring hands-on experience in [verified skills], a focus on correctness and maintainability, and a way of working that includes testing, collaboration and learning from incidents.”

### HR-08. What are your strengths?

**EN template:** “A strength of mine is [true strength]. For example, on [real project], I used it to [action and evidence].”

Nêu 1–2 strengths, mỗi strength có một proof.

## Teamwork & ownership

### HR-09. Describe a technical disagreement.

**STAR outline:** disagreement about SQL performance, caching or data ownership → clarify constraints → compare alternatives with evidence → team decision → lessons. Đề bài giả định; **chọn một sự kiện thật** trước khi dùng trong phỏng vấn.

### HR-10. Tell me about a production incident.

**EN template:** “We noticed [real impact]. I investigated [specific signals], worked on [mitigation], and verified recovery using [observations]. Afterward, we changed [prevention].”

Nếu chưa từng xử lý production incident, trung thực nói về debugging/testing scenario khác.

### HR-11. Tell me about a mistake you made.

**Structure:** acknowledge mistake → impact → fix → safeguards → reflection. Không nói “I never make mistakes”.

### HR-12. How do you handle deadlines?

**EN:** “I clarify critical requirements, separate must-have correctness from negotiable scope, communicate risks early, and agree on safe release steps with the team.”

### HR-13. How do you handle code review feedback?

**EN:** “I try to understand the risk behind the comment, confirm the behavior with tests or evidence, and either improve the code or explain my trade-off respectfully.”

### HR-14. Have you mentored other engineers?

**EN template:** “I've helped colleagues with [real technical topic], by discussing the problem, reviewing their approach, and letting them verify the fix. I'd measure success by their ability to handle similar cases independently.”

Chỉ sử dụng nếu đã thực sự mentoring.

### HR-15. What part of the system did you personally build?

**EN:** “The overall platform was a team effort. My direct responsibility was [actual service/feature/query]. I coordinated with [team/dependency] and verified [tests/results].”

### HR-16. How do you prioritize multiple requests?

**EN:** “I first consider user impact, data correctness, security, and dependencies. I clarify urgency and communicate trade-offs before committing to a delivery sequence.”

## Feedback, growth & uncertainty

### HR-17. What's your biggest weakness?

**EN template:** “One area I'm actively improving is [real skill]. I'm working on it by [specific habit or practice], and I track progress through [evidence].”

Tránh “I'm a perfectionist” như câu trả lời mặc định.

### HR-18. Tell me about something you learned quickly.

**STAR outline:** problem unfamiliar → find trusted docs/implementation → build bounded test → validate with peers → explain limitation. Chọn một việc đã thực hiện thật.

### HR-19. What if you're asked about a technology you've never used?

**EN:** “I haven't operated that technology in production. I understand [what you truly know], and I'd validate the operational requirements, build a small lab, and review failure scenarios before choosing it.”

### HR-20. What would you do differently in a previous design?

**EN template:** “With hindsight, I'd reconsider [real decision] because [new evidence]. I'd compare [alternative] and test [risk] before changing the architecture.”

## Offer, logistics & candidate questions

### HR-21. What salary do you expect?

**EN template:** “I'd like to understand the full scope and total compensation package. My current expectation is [genuine range/currency] based on the responsibilities, and I'm open to discussing the overall fit.”

Không tự bịa range, số lương cũ hoặc cam kết.

### HR-22. When can you join?

**EN template:** “My notice period is [confirmed duration]. I can confirm the earliest date once I align my handover.”

### HR-23. Do you have any questions for us?

Hỏi 2–3 câu thực chất: “What are the main technical challenges for this team?” “How are design decisions reviewed?” “How do you measure reliability and success in the first 90 days?”

### HR-24. Do you prefer individual contribution or leadership?

**EN:** “I value hands-on problem-solving and collaboration. I can contribute through implementation, reviews and technical discussions. The balance should depend on the team's needs and responsibilities.”

Không nói đã people-manage một team nếu chưa từng làm.

## Behavioral và STAR(R)

Trả lời bằng một tình huống có thật:

| Phần | Tự hỏi |
| --- | --- |
| Situation | Vấn đề gì và ảnh hưởng ai? |
| Task | Mình chịu trách nhiệm riêng phần nào? |
| Action | Mình đã làm gì, lựa chọn kỹ thuật nào và tại sao? |
| Result | Quan sát/test/metric nào xác nhận outcome? |
| Reflection | Sau đó đổi điều gì trong cách làm? |

### Ví dụ STAR — hypothetical, không phải kinh nghiệm thật

**Question:** Tell me about a technical disagreement.

**Answer:** “In a sample team scenario, an endpoint becomes slow and the team debates caching versus indexing. My responsibility would be to clarify freshness requirements and capture a query plan. I'd compare the index change against a cache-aside approach under realistic load. I'd recommend the option that meets latency and correctness targets with lower operational cost, document the decision, and monitor regression. The lesson is to evaluate evidence rather than choosing the most familiar technology.”

Không đọc ví dụ giả định như câu chuyện cá nhân nếu chưa xảy ra.

## Chuẩn bị ba mẩu chuyện cá nhân thật

- Một **problem-solving story** có code/SQL/telemetry.
- Một **teamwork or mentoring story** có đóng góp cá nhân.
- Một **mistake/failure/recovery story** có hành động và bài học.
